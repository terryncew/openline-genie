### 1. Product Hunt / Hacker News Launch Headline & Tagline
- **Title**: **ClearCovenant — powered by OpenLine** — A local-first, source-grounded HOA companion for homeowners and volunteer boards.

### 2. Plain-Language Consumer Launch Post (Developer & Homeowner Audience)
> Living in an HOA shouldn't require a law degree or a $2,000/month management contract just to know whether a 6-foot backyard fence is allowed or why your reserves are 17.5% funded.
>
> According to the [South Carolina Department of Consumer Affairs 2026 HOA Complaint Report](https://consumer.sc.gov/news/2026-02/2026-homeowners-association-complaint-report-released), the top three HOA disputes are covenant enforcement conflicts (16.8%), maintenance responsibilities (11.7%), and ignored requests to inspect records (9.5%)—and 19% of complaints close "Undetermined" because the documentary facts are disputed. Meanwhile, every major HOA software platform is sold top-down to management companies or boards, leaving individual homeowners with 1.4-star portals and zero tools of their own.
>
> Today we're preparing **ClearCovenant**:
> - **Works for 1 homeowner on day one**: Upload your CC&Rs, violation notice, or budget. Get a plain-language answer citing the exact provision and state statute (`CA Davis-Stirling + AB 130`, `FL Ch. 718/720`, `TX Prop. Code Ch. 209`, `SC HOA Act`).
> - **Honest when evidence is missing**: If your documents don't cover a question, it says *"The documents don’t establish an answer."* and drafts a statutory records request instead of guessing.
> - **Built for volunteer boards & communities too**: Compare contractor bids without losing disqualified quotes, audit reserve shortfalls, and export your entire community ledger in one click when changing managers.
> - **No automated HOA dictator**: AI assists; people decide. ClearCovenant never auto-issues fines, files liens, runs binding elections, or sends anything to anyone. Your records stay on your own computer.
