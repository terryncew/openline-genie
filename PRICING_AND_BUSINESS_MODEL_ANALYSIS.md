### 3. Measurable Outcomes That Justify Switching or Paying
1. **For a Homeowner**: Avoiding or overturning a single unlawful `$250–$350` fine (under `CA Civ. Code § 5850` `$100` cap or `S.C. Code § 27-30-130` recording rule) or recovering `$50/day` statutory records damages (`Fla. Stat. § 720.303(5)`) immediately saves **$150 to $500+**, justifying the free download and optional `$9` certified-mail fulfillment.
2. **For a Self-Managed Volunteer Board**: Replacing a `$1,500–$2,500/mo` (`$18,000–$30,000/yr`) management contract or a `$2,304/yr` Buildium subscription while cutting board administrative prep time by `5–8 hours/month` saves **$1,956 to $29,652 per year**.
