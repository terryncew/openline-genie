What the labels mean

Accepted — saved in the engine and signed by the receiver; this is the real project. Preview (dashed ring on the graph) — an exploratory change that is not accepted yet; it changes nothing until you accept it. Awaiting decision — preview parts waiting for accept or reject. Recall decision — re-checks an accepted part and anything built on it. Receiver verified — the receiver checked the project's signed receipt against the key pinned when the project loaded. ±7% — a stated planning margin in the bakery assumptions, not a measured error. Your goal — the original request you typed; the number on it is the project's current output in loaves/day — while a preview is active it shows the preview output, not the accepted baseline (the accepted number stays in the counts line and the preview banner). Graph numbers are loaves/day: a part's number is its capacity (what that part could pass); only the current limit's number equals the project's output.
