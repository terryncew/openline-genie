3. Hard Safety, Financial & Execution Provenance Limitations (Never Overclaim)

  1. Demonstration A (Service Quoting & Invoicing) Honesty Boundary: - Produces real, usable quotes, cent-accurate totals, numbered invoices, local persistence, and printable/portable exports at $0 subscription cost. - Does NOT generate customer leads, process live credit-card/ACH payments, file taxes, or guarantee business revenue.
  2. Demonstration B (Kids Robot Academy) & Robotics Safety Boundary: - Teaches movement, sensor logic, obstacle clearance, and workcell permit concepts in a free 2D browser simulator. - Application-level permit revocation (STOP) does NOT establish physical emergency stopping, safety-rated isolation (ISO 13849 / IEC 61508), or real robot hardware actuation.
  3. Model & Worker Execution Provenance: - In this offline $0-spend sandbox, OpenLineGenieEngine and GenieAppSynthesizer execute deterministic local synthesis, AST/invariant self-tests, and OS subprocess receiver verification (LOCAL_AUTONOMOUS_COMPILER_AND_VERIFIER). No scripted or deterministic step is ever represented as a live paid cloud LLM call.
  4. Cold-Extraction Packaging Defect Found & Resolved in Inherited Shared-Receiver Bundle: - During Milestone 1 verification of the inherited shared-receiver bundle, we found that a module expected a source path outside the ZIP root. In openline-genie-001.zip, all core modules in core/ (openline_guard.py, ipc_protocol.py, recall.py, tc_controller_v2.py, acs.py) run 100% self-contained from a cold unzip with zero external path dependencies.
