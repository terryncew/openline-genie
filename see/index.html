A real browser session, recorded in one continuous take: the Hearth & Crumb bakery goes from 174 to 280 loaves a day, a signed receipt is recorded, and STOP freezes changes. 52 seconds, silent.

About this recording. This demo was recorded in one continuous take against the cool-finish v0.7.1 build: the browser tab title visible in the recording reads “Genie • Living World v0.7.1”. 52 seconds, silent.

What happens when

  • ~0 s — Build starts: “Design a small neighborhood bakery.”
  • ~5 s — Living World loads. Baseline 174 loaves/day; the oven is the limit.
  • ~15 s — Discovery: another oven, 174 → 240; the combination with a shift change, 174 → 280.
  • ~20–25 s — An assumption edit is previewed, then rejected. Accepted work stays at 174.
  • ~35–36 s — The combination is accepted. Baseline becomes 280 loaves/day; the limit moves to Ingredients.
  • ~44 s — Receipt scene: the receiver record, receipt 654442a6dd6bea5e6966791c459b75a1, status open · RECEIVER_VERIFIED.
  • ~49 s — STOP engaged: the “Clear stop — allow changes” control appears and the receiver refuses further mutations; accepted work stays at 280 loaves/day.

The numbers are deterministic projections from stated assumptions, not measured revenue. The receipt proves what the receiver recorded and signed — not what happened afterward, and not business outcomes.
