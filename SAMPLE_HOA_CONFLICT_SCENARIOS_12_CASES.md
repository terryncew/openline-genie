# ClearCovenant — 12 Dogfooded Realistic HOA Conflict Scenarios

All 12 adversarial scenarios in `core/neighborhood.py` are reproducible via `python3 run_neighborhood_001.py` and selectable in 1 click inside `/neighborhood`:

| ID | Scenario & Jurisdiction | Core Conflict Tested | Verified Reference Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`SCENARIO-01-FENCE-CONFLICT`** | Fence Height Restriction (`CA`, Homeowner) | 2024 unrecorded Board Bulletin (4-ft max) vs. Recorded 2018 CC&Rs Art. VI § 6.2 (6-ft cedar allowed) | Recorded CC&Rs Art. VI § 6.2 controls over conflicting board bulletin under [Cal. Civ. Code § 4205 & § 4765](https://www.davis-stirling.com/HOME/Statutes/Civil-Code-4765). |
| **`SCENARIO-02-SELECTIVE-ENFORCEMENT`** | Selective Enforcement & Unrecorded Rule (`SC`, Homeowner) | `$250` fine under Sept 2025 board rule never recorded by Jan 10, 2026; 3 neighbors have identical stone edging | Unrecorded rule is unenforceable under [S.C. Code § 27-30-130](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c030.php); Declaration Art. IV § 4.4 bars selective enforcement. |
| **`SCENARIO-03-DISPUTED-FINE-AB130`** | Disputed `$350` Fine & Pre-Hearing Cure (`CA`, Homeowner) | Immediate `$350` fine + `$35` late fee for trash bins with no hearing notice after same-day cure | Violates [Cal. Civ. Code § 5850](https://www.davis-stirling.com/HOME/Statutes/Civil-Code-5850) (`$100` AB 130 cap, no late fees on fines) and `§ 5855` (cure before hearing bars discipline). |
| **`SCENARIO-04-LANDSCAPING-DROUGHT`** | Drought-Tolerant Landscaping (`CA`, Homeowner) | Citation under expired 2019 80%-turf rule vs. 2023 CC&R xeriscape amendment & `Civ.
